# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be welcoming, patient, constructive, and specific. Assume good intent, accept correction gracefully, respect differing learning needs, and focus criticism on ideas and code.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, deliberate intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the Garcia Systems project maintainers through the repository's published contact channel. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect privacy where possible, and may edit, reject, or remove contributions or restrict participation. Maintainers must apply these standards fairly and in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
